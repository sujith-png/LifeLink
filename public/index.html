🩺 How LifeLink Works

1. Post Request

Specify blood group, units, hospital, and urgency level with required medical requisition slip.

2. Targeted Matching

Our algorithm alerts strictly eligible donors (>90 days recovery) within proximity using ABO compatibility.

3. Secure Connection

Requester contact is released only when a donor confirms "I can come", preserving donor & requester privacy.
